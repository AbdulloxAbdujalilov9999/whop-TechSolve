How the check works

What does the officer ask?

The check has two parts.

1. Interview in English

  • Where you're coming from and going
  • Hours on duty today
  • Showing your logbook or ELD
  • Your CDL and medical card
  • Shipping papers (bill of lading)
  • Your truck and equipment

2. Road signs

  • Standard signs: weigh station, no trucks, low clearance and more
  • Electronic message boards: crash ahead, lane closed, detour
  • You explain what each sign means

If you fail the interview, the officer doesn't go on to the signs: you're placed out of service.

Source: FMCSA internal enforcement policy (May 2025) and CVSA out-of-service criteria from 25 June 2025.
